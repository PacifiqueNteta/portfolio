My Story

My path into data began on the factory floor, not behind a computer. As an Industrial Engineer, I learned to map processes, identify bottlenecks, and eliminate inefficiencies; essentially practicing data analysis long before I had dashboards to visualize it.

The turning point came during an inventory control internship, where I discovered how critical numbers(data) are to decision-making: classifying stock as runner, repeater, or stranger; knowing when to reorder; and balancing quantities. That curiosity about the "why" behind those choices led me to pursue the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate, the formal start of my data journey.
